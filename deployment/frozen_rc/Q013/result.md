## Problem Statement
The feasibility of predicting the emergence of novel pandemics is limited by the lack of pre-spillover data streams required by traditional statistical models. Current evidence supports forecasting the trajectory of identified epidemics (e.g., COVID-19) but does not validate the prediction of zoonotic spillover events before human-to-human transmission is established. The core challenge is distinguishing between post-emergence trajectory forecasting and pre-emergence detection, and determining if adaptive models can detect operational regime shifts (sustained spread) earlier than static baselines.
